## Overview

Azerbaijan is a Caspian Sea oil-and-gas economy whose resource wealth is channeled through the
**State Oil Fund (SOFAZ)**, a sovereign wealth fund established to manage petroleum revenue. A
2026 working paper by [Isgandarov & Mustafayev](/wiki/isgandarov-mustafayev-azerbaijan-public-goods/)
examines the country's "paradox of plenty" — finding that SOFAZ provides ample fiscal capacity,
but that public-goods delivery is constrained more by institutional quality (public investment
management, fiscal-rule credibility, project-selection discipline) than by available funding, a
finding relevant to the wiki's broader [sovereign wealth fund](/wiki/sovereign-wealth-fund/)
coverage.
